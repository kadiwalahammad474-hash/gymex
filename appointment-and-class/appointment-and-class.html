Frequently Asked Questions

What is gym class scheduling software?

Gym class scheduling software helps fitness businesses create and manage class timetables, trainer schedules, session availability, and member bookings from a centralized system.

What is gym class booking software?

Gym class booking software allows gyms and fitness businesses to manage class reservations and give members a convenient way to book available sessions.

Can members book gym classes online?

Yes. Members can view available classes and appointments and reserve suitable sessions through connected member-facing tools such as an app or online portal.

Can Gymex manage personal trainer schedules?

Yes. Gymex can help organize trainer schedules, personal training sessions, appointments, and other scheduled activities as part of your overall gym management process.

Can gym booking software help reduce no-shows?

A structured booking process can help gyms maintain accurate booking records and support reminder and follow-up workflows. Connecting booking information with attendance can also help identify missed sessions.

Can class bookings be connected with attendance?

Yes. Connecting booking and attendance information allows your team to see the difference between scheduled sessions and actual participation, giving you a clearer picture of member engagement.
