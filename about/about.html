ABOUT GYMEX

Built to Make Fitness Management Easier

Gymex is an all-in-one gym and fitness management software designed to help fitness businesses manage their everyday operations from one connected platform.

OUR STORY

Helping Fitness Businesses Work Smarter Since 2016

Gymex was created in 2016 with a simple goal: make managing a gym easier.

We saw how fitness businesses were relying on spreadsheets, paperwork and multiple disconnected tools to manage their daily operations. This made everyday tasks more complicated and left owners and teams with less time to focus on their members and business growth.

That inspired us to build Gymex — a connected platform designed around the everyday needs of gyms and fitness businesses.

Over the years, Gymex has continued to evolve with new features, integrations and automation while staying focused on what matters most: making fitness business management simpler.

OUR BELIEF

Technology Should Make Your Business Easier, Not More Complicated

We believe good software should simplify everyday work. That's why Gymex focuses on bringing essential fitness business operations into one place — from managing members and leads to scheduling classes, tracking attendance, handling payments, communicating with members and understanding business performance.

Our goal is simple: reduce repetitive administrative work and give fitness businesses better control over their operations.
