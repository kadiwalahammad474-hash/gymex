Gym Marketing Software That Attracts, Engages & Retains Members

Attract new members, keep existing members engaged, and reconnect with inactive members using smarter, data-driven communication. Gymex helps you manage targeted communication with leads and members from one connected platform.

Marketing That Connects to Your Gym Operations

Marketing is more effective when it is based on what is actually happening at your gym.

With Gymex, your team can use member and lead information to communicate at important moments: when someone enquires, joins, books a class, misses visits, has a payment due, or approaches a membership renewal.

Instead of sending the same message to everyone, you can create more relevant communication for the people who need it.

Use Gymex to support marketing around:

New enquiries and trial follow-ups
New-member welcome messages
Payment due and renewal reminders
Class booking confirmations and updates
Gym offers, events, challenges, and announcements
Birthday and membership-Anniversary wishes
Inactive-member re-engagement
Referral and upgrade campaigns

Frequently Asked Questions

What is gym marketing software?

Gym marketing software helps fitness businesses manage campaigns, member communication, lead follow-ups, promotions, and retention activity. It can connect marketing messages with member data, attendance, memberships, bookings, and payments.

Can Gymex send WhatsApp, SMS, email, and push notifications?

Gymex can support these communication channels when the relevant WhatsApp, SMS, email, or push-notification service is integrated or enabled as an additional service.

Can I send renewal and payment reminders to members?

Yes. Gymex helps your team track upcoming renewals, payment due dates, and outstanding dues. When a supported communication service is enabled, your team can send manual or automated reminders through the available channel.

Can I target inactive gym members with a campaign?

Yes. Your team can use member attendance, membership status, and payment information to identify members who have not visited recently or whose plans are expiring.

Is gym marketing software useful for lead follow-up?

Yes. Gym marketing software helps teams communicate with prospects after an enquiry, trial booking, or visit. When connected with lead management, it helps staff keep follow-ups organized from first enquiry through membership sign-up.

Can I promote gym classes, offers, and events with Gymex?

Yes. Gymex can help your team communicate promotions, class updates, offers, events, fitness challenges, and important gym announcements to the relevant audience through enabled communication channels.
