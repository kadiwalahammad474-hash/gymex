PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 2024

Protecting your private information is our top priority. This Statement of Privacy applies to the Gymex's website and governs data collection and usage. By using the Gymex's website, you consent to the data practices described in this statement. We are very sensitive to your privacy.

Collection of your Personal Information

Gymex may collect personally identifiable information, such as your e-mail address, name, home or work address or telephone number. This information is solely used for the smooth functioning of product handover. Gymex will not delete customer information for paying members. However, Gymex reserves the right to delete customer information for members with a free account status at any time or members who have not renewed their services from the last 3 months.

Information about your computer hardware and software also may be automatically collected by Gymex. This information can include: your IP address, browser type, domain names, access times and referring website addresses. This information is used by Gymex for the operation of the service, to maintain quality of the services, and to provide general statistics regarding use of the Gymex website.

Use of your Personal Information

Gymex collects and uses your personal information to operate its website(s) and deliver the services you have requested. Gymex may also use your personally identifiable information to inform you of other products or services available from Gymex and its affiliates.

Sharing Information with Third Parties

Gymex does not sell, rent or lease its customer lists to third parties. Gymex may share data with trusted partners to help perform statistical analysis, send you email or postal mail, provide customer support, or arrange for deliveries. All such third parties are prohibited from using your personal information except to provide these services to Gymex, and they are required to maintain the confidentiality of your information.

Use of Cookies

The Gymex website may use "cookies" to help you personalize your online experience. A cookie is a text file that is placed on your hard disk by a web page server. Cookies cannot be used to run programs or deliver viruses to your computer. Cookies are uniquely assigned to you, and can only be read by a web server in the domain that issued the cookie to you.

One of the primary purposes of cookies is to provide a convenience feature to save you time. The purpose of a cookie is to tell the Web server that you have returned to a specific page. For example, if you personalize Gymex pages, or register with Gymex site or services, a cookie helps Gymex to recall your specific information on subsequent visits.

You have the ability to accept or decline cookies. Most Web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer. If you choose to decline cookies, you may not be able to fully experience the interactive features of the Gymex services or websites you visit.

Security of your Personal Information

Gymex secures your personal information from unauthorized access, use or disclosure. When sensitive personal information is transmitted to other websites, it is protected through the use of encryption, such as the Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) protocol.

You are responsible for safeguarding and preventing unauthorized access to the user information and password that you use to access the Gymex website. You agree not to disclose your password to any third party and you are responsible for any activity using your account, whether or not you authorized that activity. You must immediately notify Gymex of any unauthorized use of your account.

Children Under Thirteen

Gymex does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of thirteen. If you are under the age of thirteen, you must ask your parent or guardian for permission to use this website.

Changes to this Statement

Gymex will occasionally update this Statement of Privacy to reflect company and customer feedback. Gymex encourages you to periodically review this Statement to be informed of how Gymex is protecting your information.
